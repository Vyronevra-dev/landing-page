Meloflix Music App

Your music, your way

Meloflix is a browser-based music player that plays your local MP3 files with a clean, Spotify-inspired experience. No subscriptions, no streaming limits, no data collection. Just upload your songs and enjoy full playback controls, automatic album art, shuffle and repeat modes, all from your browser.



Built as a Progressive Web App, Meloflix can be installed directly on your desktop or phone without visiting an app store. It works fully offline, remembers nothing about you, and puts you in complete control of your listening experience. Your music lives on your device, and so does Meloflix.

Features it has

  • Repeat Mode
  • Shuffle
  • Pause & Play
  • Plays songs offline
  • Free
  • Dark UI
